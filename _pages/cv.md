## Andreas Elben

I am a tenure-track scientist at the [Paul Scherrer Institute (PSI)](https://www.psi.ch/en), working in the [Laboratory for Theoretical and Computational Physics](https://www.psi.ch/en/ltc) and affiliated with the [ETHZ-PSI Quantum Computing Hub](https://www.psi.ch/en/lnq/qchub).

My research interests include quantum many-body dynamics, randomized measurements, quantum simulation, and the verification, probing, and benchmarking of intermediate-scale quantum devices.
