Our research activities are embedded in the [Group of Computational Physics](https://www.psi.ch/en/ltc/computergestutzte-physik) within the [Laboratory for Theoretical and Computational Physics](https://www.psi.ch/en/ltc) at the [Paul Scherrer Institute](https://www.psi.ch/en), part of the [PSI Center for Scientific Computing, Theory and Data](https://www.psi.ch/en/csd).

We work closely with the [ETHZ-PSI Quantum Computing Hub](https://www.psi.ch/en/lnq/qchub). We are part of a strong quantum effort at the [Paul Scherrer Institute (PSI)](https://www.psi.ch/en), with experimental groups working on the trapped-ions (Cornelius Hempel, PSI),  superconducting-qubits (Andreas Wallraff, ETH Zürich and PSI), bosonic quantum information (Alexander Grimm, PSI), and Rydberg-atom-arrays (Wenchao Xu, ETH Zürich and PSI).

## [Andreas Elben](https://www.psi.ch/de/ltc/people/andreas-elben)

Tenure-track scientist, [Laboratory for Theoretical and Computational Physics](https://www.psi.ch/en/ltc) and [ETHZ-PSI Quantum Computing Hub](https://www.psi.ch/en/lnq/qchub), [Paul Scherrer Institute](https://www.psi.ch/en)

[PSI profile](https://www.psi.ch/de/ltc/people/andreas-elben) · [CV](/cv/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=_QUD5LQAAAAJ&hl=en) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/search/quant-ph?searchtype=author&query=Elben,+A) · [GitHub](https://github.com/andreaselben)

I work at the interface of quantum information theory, many-body physics, and quantum simulation, with a focus on methods for characterizing, learning, and benchmarking complex quantum systems.

Before joining PSI, I was a Walter Burke Postdoctoral Fellow at the [Institute for Quantum Information and Matter](https://iqim.caltech.edu/) at [Caltech](https://www.caltech.edu/). I obtained my PhD in physics from the [University of Innsbruck](https://www.uibk.ac.at/en/) and the [Institute for Quantum Optics and Quantum Information (IQOQI)](https://www.oeaw.ac.at/iqoqi/innsbruck). For more details, see [CV](/cv/).
